# PRIMAVEX INTERNATIONAL LTD --- Product Requirements Document

## 1. Project Overview

**Brand:** Primavex International Ltd\
**Business type:** Agricultural commodity sourcing and export\
**Primary market:** International buyers\
**Primary website goal:** Establish credibility, present export products
clearly, qualify interested buyers, and generate sample and bulk-order
enquiries.

Primavex is positioned as an export business supplying locally sourced
agricultural products to international customers. The business currently
relies on third-party logistics providers for international shipment
rather than operating its own logistics network.

The website is therefore a **B2B/B2C export lead-generation and trust
platform**, not a conventional online shop.

## 2. Product Scope

Products stated in the initial client brief:

-   Cocoa
-   Sesame
-   Coffee
-   A fourth product described verbally by the client as "Ebisco"

**Important:** "Ebisco" must be confirmed with the client before the

-   Shipping/export documentation available
-   Sample availability
-   Lead time
-   Price/quotation method

## 3. Problem

International buyers need confidence before committing money to an
unfamiliar supplier.

The website must reduce the buyer's uncertainty around:

1.  Whether Primavex is a real business.
2.  What products are actually available.
3.  Whether the products meet the buyer's required quality.
4.  Whether the supplier can handle the required quantity.
5.  Whether buyers can test the product before a bulk order.
6.  How shipping is handled.
7.  How to contact Primavex and request a quotation.

## 4. Target Users

### Persona A --- International Business Buyer

**Profile:** Importer, distributor, wholesaler, food manufacturer,
commodity trader, retailer, or procurement manager.

**Needs:** - Reliable supplier - Clear product specifications -
Consistent quality - Bulk quantities - Transparent enquiry process -
Sample before committing - Shipping information - Fast communication -
Professional documentation

**Primary action:** Request a sample or bulk quotation.

### Persona B --- Individual International Buyer

**Profile:** Individual customer interested in purchasing agricultural
products for personal, small-business, or specialised use.

**Needs:** - Product authenticity - Quality confidence - Sample option -
Simple enquiry process - Clear communication about quantity and shipping

**Primary action:** Request a sample or enquire about availability.

## 5. Core User Journey

### Sample-first journey

1.  Buyer discovers Primavex.
2.  Buyer understands what Primavex exports.
3.  Buyer views a product.
4.  Buyer reviews available product information.
5.  Buyer chooses **Request a Sample**.
6.  Buyer submits contact and product requirements.
7.  Primavex contacts the buyer.
8.  A small test quantity is arranged and shipped through an appropriate
    logistics partner.
9.  Buyer evaluates authenticity and quality.
10. Buyer proceeds to a bulk-order discussion if satisfied.

### Bulk-order journey

1.  Buyer discovers Primavex.
2.  Buyer selects a product.
3.  Buyer reviews product information.
4.  Buyer selects **Request Bulk Quote**.
5.  Buyer submits required quantity, destination, and contact details.
6.  Primavex reviews the enquiry.
7.  Primavex provides availability, pricing, shipping, and next steps.
8.  Buyer proceeds to commercial agreement.

## 6. Website Goals

### Primary goals

-   Build trust with international buyers.
-   Present Primavex as a credible export supplier.
-   Showcase available agricultural commodities.
-   Explain the sample-to-bulk purchasing process.
-   Generate qualified enquiries.
-   Make requesting samples and quotations easy.
-   Communicate that international delivery is available through
    logistics partners.

### Secondary goals

-   Create a professional digital identity.
-   Improve discoverability through search.
-   Provide a foundation for future export/logistics capabilities.
-   Support future expansion into more products and markets.

## 7. Non-Goals for MVP

The first version should NOT include:

-   Full ecommerce checkout
-   Automated international shipping calculation
-   Live inventory management
-   Customer accounts
-   Online payment processing
-   Automated customs calculations
-   Proprietary logistics tracking
-   Claims that Primavex owns its shipping/logistics network
-   Unverified certifications or quality claims

## 9. Homepage Requirements

### Hero

The hero should immediately communicate:

**What Primavex does:**\
Supplies and exports quality agricultural commodities from Nigeria to
international buyers.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Sample\
**Secondary CTA:** Explore Products

The visual direction should feel premium, international, trustworthy,
and agricultural without looking like a generic farm website.

### Product Showcase

Show confirmed products using high-quality product photography.

Each product card should communicate: - Product name - Short
descriptor - Availability/status if confirmed - View Product CTA

### Trust Section

Potential trust points, subject to client verification:

-   Quality-focused sourcing
-   Sample-before-bulk process
-   International delivery coordination
-   Responsive communication
-   Flexible order discussions

Do not publish numerical claims, certifications, countries served, years
of experience, or capacity figures until the client verifies them.

### Sample Process

This should be one of the strongest sections because the sample process
reduces buyer risk.

Suggested structure:

**01 --- Choose a product**\
Tell us what commodity you need.

**02 --- Request a sample**\
Receive a small quantity for evaluation.

**03 --- Confirm quality**\
Assess the product before committing to a bulk order.

**04 --- Place your bulk order**\
Agree quantity, pricing, packaging, and delivery details.

**05 --- We coordinate delivery**\
Primavex works with logistics partners to arrange international
shipment.

## 13. Trust & Compliance Requirements

The website must not imply that Primavex owns logistics operations if it
currently uses third-party logistics providers.

Preferred wording:

> "We coordinate international delivery through trusted logistics
> partners."

Avoid:

> "Our global logistics network"

unless the client can substantiate ownership or control of such a
network.

All certifications, inspection claims, quality standards, quantities,
country coverage, testimonials, company registration claims, and
statistics must be supplied or approved by the client before
publication.
