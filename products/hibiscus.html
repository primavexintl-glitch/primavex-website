Hibiscus Flowers

Premium dried flowers, Export ready.

Dried hibiscus flowers (Roselle calyx) packed with natural ruby-red pigmentation, high acidity, and vibrant character, sourced directly for global beverage, tea, and pharmaceutical manufacturers.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS

  • Origin: Nigeria (Kano, Katsina, Jigawa, Bauchi)
  • Grades: Grade A Whole Flower (≥ 80% to 85% whole calyx, ≤ 5% broken)
  • Purity: 98.0% to 99.0% min
  • Foreign Matter: ≤ 1.0% to 2.0% max
  • Moisture Content: 8.0% to 10.0% max (Contracts up to 11% to 12%)
  • Packaging: New PP bags with inner polyethylene moisture liner (25kg / 50kg bags)
  • MOQ: Determined by the client

OVERVIEW

Product Overview

Nigerian hibiscus (Hibiscus sabdariffa / Roselle) is globally renowned for having deep anthocyanin red colouration and high natural acidity. Primavex inspects and cleans each shipment to ensure clean, residue-free whole flowers.

SAMPLE AVAILABILITY

Samples are available upon request. Submit a sample request and our team will confirm availability and coordinate shipment.

Request a Sample →

Harvesting & Handling

Carefully hand-stripped from mature plants after flowering, gently air-dried to preserve vibrant antioxidant pigments, and passed through vibratory sieves to eliminate dust, fine debris, and broken fragments.

Commercial Applications

Essential ingredient for herbal and fruit tea blenders, RTD beverage bottling, natural food colouring extraction, syrup and cordial making, and cosmetic botanical extracts.

Export Logistics & Packaging

Packed into breathable poly-lined bags (25kg or 50kg) to avoid transit moisture condensation. Shipped in 20ft (approx. 12 to 14 MT) or 40ft High Cube containers (approx. 25 MT).
