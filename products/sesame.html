Sesame Seeds

Clean, sorted, export-ready.

Hulled and natural sesame seeds from trusted Nigerian farms. Pure and consistently graded for international food and oil crushing markets.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS

  • Origin: Nigeria (Benue, Kano, Jigawa, Nasarawa regions)
  • Purity: 98.0% to 99.5% min (Grade A / Sortex Clean 99.5% available)
  • Oil Content: ≥ 45% to 52%
  • Moisture: 8.0% max (Sortex target: ≤ 6.0%)
  • Foreign Matter: ≤ 2.0% max (Sortex: ≤ 0.5%)
  • Packaging: Clean PP or multi-wall paper bags (45kg / 50kg bags)
  • MOQ: Determined by the client

OVERVIEW

Product Overview

Nigerian sesame seeds are highly prized globally for their rich nutty flavour, light uniform colour, and high oil extraction yield. Primavex delivers reliably graded batches suited for food processing and industrial oil extraction.

SAMPLE AVAILABILITY

Samples are available upon request. Submit a sample request and our team will confirm availability and coordinate shipment.

Request a Sample →

Sourcing & Processing

Sourced directly from established agricultural belts across Northern and Central Nigeria. Processed through multi-stage destoners, gravity separators, and optical color sorting (Sortex) to remove dust, stalks, and unripened seeds.

Commercial Applications

Widely utilised in bakery and confectionery topping, tahini and halva manufacturing, culinary sesame oil extraction, hummus production, and international seasoning blends.

Export Logistics & Packaging

Packed in robust new woven polypropylene (PP) bags of 50kg nett with fumigation and phytosanitary certification. Shipped in 20ft dry containers holding approx. 18 to 20 metric tons.
