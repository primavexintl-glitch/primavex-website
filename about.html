ABOUT PRIMAVEX

Built to connect African quality with global opportunity.

WHO WE ARE

A serious sourcing and export partner.

Primavex International Ltd is a Nigerian agricultural commodity sourcing and export company. We specialise in connecting locally grown, quality-verified commodities: cocoa, sesame, coffee, and hibiscus with international buyers.

We work with a network of trusted local sources and prepare commodities for export with full attention to quality, specification, and buyer expectations. International delivery is coordinated through established logistics partners.

OUR VALUES

Principles that guide every transaction.

Integrity

We operate with transparency, no hidden conditions and no inflated claims.

When we say we source quality commodities, we mean it. Our buyers can verify every claim through our sample process.

Quality First

Every commodity is assessed before it leaves our hands.

Reliability

Clear timelines and consistent communication throughout.

Global Perspective

We understand international buyer expectations and meet them.

Transparency

Clear pricing, honest updates and no surprises from sourcing to delivery.

WORKING WITH BUYERS

A process built around your confidence.

We understand that international commodity purchasing carries risk. That is why we built a sample-first approach that lets buyers verify quality independently before committing to bulk quantities.

From first enquiry to final delivery, our team maintains clear and responsive communication so you always know where your order stands.
