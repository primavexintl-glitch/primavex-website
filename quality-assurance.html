QUALITY ASSURANCE

Quality is part of every order.

We don't treat quality as a claim, we treat it as a process. Every commodity is assessed before export, and every buyer can verify quality independently through our sample-first model.

“We believe every buyer deserves to verify quality before they commit. The sample is not a formality, it is the foundation of how we do business.”
OUR STANDARDS

Six pillars of quality commitment.

01

Responsible Sourcing

We work with a trusted network of local suppliers who understand our quality expectations. Sourcing relationships are built on accountability and consistency.

02

Pre-Export Inspection

Every commodity batch is assessed before leaving the source. We evaluate grade, moisture, colour, and contamination, and only proceed when the product meets standard.

03

Sample Verification

Buyers receive a sample that directly represents the bulk commodity. The sample-first model is our commitment that what you test is what you receive.

04

Clear Specifications

We communicate product grades and specifications clearly and upfront, no ambiguity. Buyers always know what they are purchasing before confirming an order.

05

Packaging Standards

Commodities are packaged to protect quality during transit. Packaging specifications are confirmed with each buyer based on order size and destination requirements.

06

Buyer Communication

We keep buyers informed throughout the process, from sample confirmation to bulk order status and delivery coordination updates.

DOCUMENTATION

Documentation and certifications.

We provide buyers with the product documentation required for import, compliance, and internal verification. Specific documentation availability is confirmed at order stage.

CERTIFICATIONS

Certification information to be added as Primavex obtains relevant export and quality certifications.

Standard documents typically provided:

  • Certificate of Origin
  • Phytosanitary Certificate
  • Quality / Inspection Report
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading (via logistics partner)
  • Commercial Invoice
