# PRIMAVEX INTERNATIONAL LTD --- MVP Definition

## 1. MVP Objective

Build a polished, trustworthy export website that allows an
international buyer to:

1.  Understand what Primavex supplies.
2.  Verify that the business presents itself professionally.
3.  Learn how the sample-first process works.
4.  View product information.
5.  Request a sample.
6.  Request a bulk quotation.
7.  Contact Primavex directly.

The MVP should prioritise **trust and qualified enquiries over ecommerce
functionality**.

## 2. MVP Pages

### 1. Home

Required: - Navigation - Hero - Product overview - Why Primavex -
Sample-first process - Quality/trust section - CTA - Footer

### 2. Products

Required: - Product grid - Product categories/cards - Product detail
links

### 3. Product Detail

Required: - Product image - Product description - Specifications -
Packaging - MOQ, once confirmed - Sample availability - Bulk order CTA -
Shipping information - Enquiry CTA

### 4. How It Works

Required: - Choose product - Request sample - Test quality - Confirm
order - Delivery coordination

### 5. About

Required: - Company overview - Mission - Sourcing approach - Quality
commitment - Export focus

### 6. Contact / Request

Required: - Sample request - Bulk quote request - General enquiry -
Email/WhatsApp/contact details

## 3. MVP Features

### Must Have

-   Responsive website
-   Mobile navigation
-   Product pages
-   Enquiry forms
-   Sample request CTA
-   Bulk quote CTA
-   Email notification for enquiries
-   WhatsApp CTA if supplied
-   SEO basics
-   Optimised images
-   Basic analytics if approved
-   Accessible forms
-   Success/error feedback

### Nice to Have

-   FAQ
-   Testimonials
-   Certifications section
-   Downloadable product catalogue
-   Country/market selector
-   Product comparison
-   CMS for product management

### Not in MVP

-   Online checkout
-   Payment gateway
-   Customer dashboard
-   Inventory system
-   Automated logistics calculator
-   Shipment tracking
-   Automated quotation engine
-   Complex CRM
-   Multi-language support

## 4. MVP Conversion Strategy

Primary conversion:

**Request a Sample**

Secondary conversion:

**Request a Bulk Quote**

Tertiary conversion:

**Contact Primavex**

These CTAs should appear consistently throughout the site without
becoming repetitive.

## 6. MVP Acceptance Criteria

The MVP is ready when:

-   A first-time visitor understands what Primavex does within seconds.
-   A visitor can find the products without difficulty.
-   A visitor can understand the sample-first model.
-   A visitor can request a sample from at least one clear CTA.
-   A visitor can request a bulk quote.
-   Forms work on desktop and mobile.
-   The site clearly distinguishes Primavex's commodity-export role from
    third-party logistics providers.
-   No unsupported claims appear.
-   Product information is easy to scan.
-   The visual system is consistent across pages.
-   The site loads well on mobile.
-   Navigation works correctly.
-   Contact details are easy to find.

## 7. MVP Design Principle

Every major section should answer one of these buyer questions:

**What do you sell?**\
**Is the quality reliable?**\
**Can I test it first?**\
**Can you supply my quantity?**\
**How will it reach me?**\
**How do I contact you?**

If a section does not help answer one of these questions or build
credibility, it should not be prioritised in the MVP.
